# Inclusive Education Falsification and Sensitivity Plan Protocol

This workflow documents planned pretrend or baseline diagnostics, placebo event
times, negative-control outcomes and exposures, alternative comparison groups,
alternative windows and model specifications, unobserved-confounding and
missing-data sensitivity, multiple-testing strategy, and interpretation rules.

Plans should be specified before results are interpreted. Documentation prompts
identify unresolved fields but are not approval, validity, or quality decisions.

The platform does not run diagnostics or models. A favourable pretrend,
placebo, negative-control, or sensitivity result does not prove identification,
absence of bias, policy effects, or causality. No significance test, effect
estimate, pass/fail label, or automatic causal conclusion is produced.

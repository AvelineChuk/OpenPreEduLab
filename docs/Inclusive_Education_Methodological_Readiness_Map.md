# Inclusive Education Methodological Readiness Map

## Purpose

The Methodological Readiness Navigator organises the Researcher Mode workflows
into a coherent research sequence. It helps researchers locate the appropriate
prototype audit without implying that every workflow is required or that
completion establishes validity.

## Five phases

### 1. Instrument foundations

This phase covers expert content review, cognitive interviews and item
revision, instrument version registration, and feasibility testing. It asks
whether an instrument is documented and usable enough for further study.

### 2. Measurement evidence

This phase covers preliminary reliability, exploratory structure, subgroup
comparability, external relationships, weight sensitivity, and Bootstrap
sampling uncertainty. These workflows provide bounded evidence, not instrument
validation or automatic item decisions.

### 3. Longitudinal readiness

This phase covers panel linkage, attrition, paired uncertainty, timing and
fieldwork metadata, measurement comparability, analysis planning, and policy or
context event alignment. Temporal ordering does not establish improvement,
policy effects, or causality.

### 4. Policy-design readiness

This phase covers exposure definitions, identification assumptions,
falsification and sensitivity plans, and estimation specifications. The
workflows document proposed designs but do not fit models or establish causal
identification.

### 5. Reproducibility and release

This phase covers analysis reproducibility, results-reporting boundaries,
claim-evidence traceability, and release-package readiness. It supports
transparent research records without verifying findings or publishing files.

## Navigator fields

The downloadable catalog contains:

- phase;
- workflow;
- research problem;
- prototype output;
- interpretation boundary;
- protocol document.

## Interpretation

The navigator is not a readiness score, checklist approval, quality ranking,
causal pathway, or required linear sequence. Researchers should select methods
according to theory, design, data, ethics, and governance. Synthetic
demonstration data remain separate from any future governed empirical study.

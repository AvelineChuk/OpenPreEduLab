# Inclusive Education Identification-Design Readiness Protocol

This workflow records a proposed policy or context research design, including
treatment and comparison definitions, time zero, pre/post periods,
identification assumptions, confounding strategy, anticipation, interference,
baseline evidence, negative controls, sensitivity analysis, and causal-claim
status.

Supported labels are design candidates only: descriptive, interrupted time
series, difference-in-differences, matched comparison, natural experiment, and
randomized design. Selecting a label does not mean its assumptions are met.

Documentation prompts identify unresolved fields. They are not approval,
rejection, validity, bias, or quality decisions. The workflow fits no model and
does not establish exchangeability, parallel trends, valid comparison groups,
absence of interference, policy effects, counterfactual outcomes, or causality.

Future causal work requires preregistration, defensible identification,
appropriate samples, diagnostics, uncertainty, falsification, sensitivity
analysis, ethical review, and independent methodological review.

# Inclusive Education Event Exposure Definition Protocol

This protocol distinguishes a registered policy or context event from actual
institution-level exposure. The workflow records exposure scope, status,
dates, intensity definition, lag definition, comparator definition, and source
reference. It identifies unresolved documentation prompts only.

`documented_exposure` does not mean that a causal treatment has been observed.
`mixed_or_unknown` and `not_assessed` remain explicit uncertainty states.

The audit does not infer which institutions received an event, estimate dose,
choose a lag, validate a comparator, control confounding, estimate policy
effects, or establish causality. It does not produce weights, exclusions,
rankings, significance tests, or effect estimates.

Future governed studies require institution-level exposure records, verified
source provenance, preregistered intensity and lag definitions, a defensible
comparison design, sensitivity analysis, and explicit causal assumptions.

# Preschool Fiscal Source Search

## Purpose

This document records the controlled discovery of official Guangdong
preschool-related fiscal sources. It distinguishes verified raw-source
candidates from a complete, definition-compatible fiscal input for the
Preschool Resource Allocation Index (PRAI).

## Required variable

A real-data PRAI pilot requires annual public expenditure explicitly assigned
to preschool or kindergarten education, with a documented geographic scope,
accounting basis, unit, and denominator strategy. A provincial-level final
accounts value is not automatically a province-wide expenditure total or a
per-child financial-resource indicator.

## Archived official candidates

The following Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance publications have
been archived as immutable raw sources and registered for independent scope
review:

| Year | Official publication | Provisional status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2017 | Provincial-level final accounts | Independently confirmed as a narrow, single-year context reference only |
| 2019 | Provincial-level final-accounts draft (statements) | Pending independent scope review |
| 2020 | Provincial-level final-accounts draft (statements) | Pending independent scope review |

Each source visibly reports a `学前教育` field in a provincial-level general
public-budget expenditure final-accounts table. The field must not be staged,
aggregated into a Guangdong-wide total, or used as a PRAI fiscal input unless a
later study design documents a definition-compatible use.

The archived official *Guangdong Statistical Yearbook 2025* was screened on
31 July 2026. Its Table 8-2, *Basic Items of Local Government General Public
Budget Revenue and Expenditure*, reports a general `教育` (Education)
expenditure row for 2010-2024 but no preschool/kindergarten sub-item. It is
registered as `GD_YEARBOOK_2025_TABLE_8_2_SCREEN` for independent confirmation
and may be used only as potential general-education-finance context, never as
preschool-specific expenditure or a PRAI fiscal input.

## Official search boundary

On 31 July 2026, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance homepage
search service (`https://search.gd.gov.cn/search/local_msg/125`) was queried
for the following terms:

1. `2018年广东省省级决算草案报表`
2. `广东省省级决算草案报表`
3. `2018年省级决算`
4. `2018年广东省省级财政决算`

The service returned no result that could be identified as an official 2018
final-accounts publication. This is a platform-search outcome only. It does
not establish that the source does not exist, and it does not authorise a
non-official substitute, interpolation, or reconstruction of a value.

## Next official channels

   archives or annual budget/final-accounts catalogues.
2. Guangdong Provincial People's Congress final-accounts materials that link
   to an official government attachment.
3. Official fiscal disclosure portals that retain the original document,
   publication date, and scope statement.

Any future candidate must be archived with its source URL, retrieval date,
SHA-256 checksum, visible table location, unit, and scope. It must then pass
independent review before any proposed analytical use is considered.

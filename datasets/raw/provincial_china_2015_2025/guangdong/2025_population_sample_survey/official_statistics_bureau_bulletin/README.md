# Guangdong 2025 Population Sample Survey Source Screening

## Source identity

- Publisher: Guangdong Provincial Bureau of Statistics
- Publication: *Main Data Bulletin of the 2025 National 1% Population Sample
  Survey in Guangdong Province* (`广东省2025年全国1％人口抽样调查主要数据公报`)
- Official page: <http://stats.gd.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/4/4919/post_4919997.html>
- Archived raw file: `official_2025_population_sample_survey_bulletin.pdf`
- SHA-256: `951017AE5404FD44179EADABDECEC8B8CBBEBF78841D8AB6CC2225A77C7A1A14`

## Screening outcome

The two-page bulletin explicitly reports only the broad `0-14` resident-
population group for children: 21.91 million people. It does not report separate
values for ages 3-5, ages 3-6, or individual ages 3, 4, and 5.

This source is therefore not definition-compatible with the project's required
preschool-age population variable. It must not be used to derive or approximate
that variable. The archive is retained as source-screening evidence only; no
value has been staged or processed.

## Verified visible content

- Attachment 1 is denominated in yuan and records a total supplementary allocation of `41,000,000.00` yuan.
- Attachment 1 allocates the supplementary central fund across listed local units. Its recorded functional classification is transfer-payment expenditure, not a province-wide historical expenditure total.
- Attachment 2 is denominated in 10,000 yuan and records an annual total of `17,430.0` (10,000 yuan), including `13,330.0` advanced and `4,100.0` supplementary funding. This annual-total scope must not be added to attachment 1.

## Permitted use and restriction

These are immutable raw-source files. They may be considered after independent review as **policy-simulation parameter candidates** for a Guangdong preschool-development-fund scenario. They are not province-wide actual preschool expenditure, not per-child fiscal input, and not directly eligible for PRAI, equity, efficiency, or causal policy evaluation. No municipality-level allocation should be merged automatically with Guangdong provincial resource series.

## Verified visible scope

The report concerns the **central support-for-preschool-education-development
transfer-payment programme** in Guangdong, not all sources of provincial
preschool expenditure. Visual review of retained pages 1 and 2 verified:

- 2025 programme funding: `169,887` ten-thousand yuan.
- Composition: `20,187` ten-thousand yuan advanced in 2024 and `149,700`
  ten-thousand yuan additionally allocated in 2025.
- End-of-2025 programme expenditure: `158,958.86` ten-thousand yuan.
- Reported execution rate: `93.57%`.
- Geographic programme coverage: 20 prefecture-level cities.

## Permitted use and restriction

The report is an official programme-execution source. After independent review,
its funding and execution indicators may be used as **policy-simulation
parameters** or to describe the execution of this specific central transfer
payment. They are not province-wide preschool public expenditure, are not
per-child fiscal input, and must not be used directly as a PRAI fiscal-resource
indicator or to claim total preschool spending.

The PDF has a limited non-searchable rendering structure. The retained page
renders provide visual evidence for the values above; future transcription must
be independently checked against the original PDF.

## Corrected source finding and use boundary

An independent review identified an error in the original source screening.
Page 6 of the PDF contains a `学前教育` (preschool education) row. In the table's
stated unit of 10,000 yuan, the row reports a 2017 budget of 4,816, an adjusted
budget of 4,816, and final accounts of 4,778.

These figures describe **Guangdong provincial-level (省本级)** expenditure only.
They are not a consolidated total for all of Guangdong Province and must not be
described as Guangdong-wide preschool public expenditure. The record is a
single-year provincial-level fiscal observation, not a complete panel.

The archived PDF remains immutable. No value from it has been copied to
`datasets/staging/` or `datasets/processed/`, and it is not a current PRAI
fiscal input. The corrected README and manifest require an independent
re-review before any narrower, source-supported research use is considered.

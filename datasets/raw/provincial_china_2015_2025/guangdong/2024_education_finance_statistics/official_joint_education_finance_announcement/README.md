## Source composition and visible scope

The four-page attachment is a raster-only scanned PDF, with no searchable text
layer. The retained page-1 render verifies the table title and the visible
columns: general-public-budget education expenditure, its year-on-year change,
its share of general-public-budget expenditure, per-student general-public-
budget education expenditure, and the latter's year-on-year change.

For Guangdong Province, the visible first-row general-public-budget education
expenditure is 398.643 billion yuan. This value is **general education
expenditure**, not preschool-specific expenditure.

## Screening outcome

The table does not display a preschool or kindergarten expenditure field on its
verified page. It therefore cannot supply the project's required P3 variable:
annual provincial public expenditure explicitly identified as preschool or
kindergarten expenditure.

The raw PDF may be considered after independent review as a context-only,
official cross-validation source for a general-education-expenditure variable.
It must not be treated as a PRAI preschool fiscal-input variable or a substitute
for `20502` / `2050201` preschool expenditure. No numerical value from this
file has been staged or processed.

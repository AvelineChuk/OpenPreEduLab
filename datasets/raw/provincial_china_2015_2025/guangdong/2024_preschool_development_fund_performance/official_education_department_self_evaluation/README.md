## Verified visible scope

This report concerns Guangdong's **2024 central support-for-preschool-
education-development transfer-payment programme**, rather than all provincial
preschool expenditure. Visual review of retained pages 1 and 2 verified:

- Programme funding: `22,430` ten-thousand yuan.
- Composition: `20,700` ten-thousand yuan advanced in 2023 and `1,730`
  ten-thousand yuan additionally allocated in 2024.
- Intended components: `5,500` ten-thousand yuan for preschool education aid
  and `16,930` ten-thousand yuan for expanding resources and improving quality.
- Programme expenditure by 31 December 2024: `11,786.21` ten-thousand yuan.
- Geographic programme coverage: 20 prefecture-level cities.

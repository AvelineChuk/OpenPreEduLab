## Verified visible scope

This report concerns Guangdong's **2022 central support-for-preschool-
education-development transfer-payment programme**, rather than all provincial
preschool expenditure. Visual review of retained pages 1 to 3 verified:

- Programme funding: `21,000` ten-thousand yuan.
- Composition: `15,570` ten-thousand yuan advanced in 2021 and `5,430`
  ten-thousand yuan additionally allocated in 2022.
- Programme expenditure by December 2022: `10,725.63` ten-thousand yuan.
- Reported execution rate: `51.07%`.

The report attributes below-schedule spending primarily to preschool project
construction procedures and pandemic-period project delays. This is a
programme-administration explanation from the source, not a causal conclusion
about Guangdong's overall preschool system.

## Permitted use and restriction

After independent review, the funding, expenditure, and execution-rate fields
may be used as **policy-simulation parameters** or to describe execution of
this specific transfer-payment programme. They are not total provincial
preschool public expenditure, per-child fiscal input, or a direct PRAI fiscal
resource indicator. Future transcription must be independently checked against
the original PDF and retained page renders.

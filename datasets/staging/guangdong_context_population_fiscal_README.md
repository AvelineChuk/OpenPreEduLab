# Guangdong Population and Fiscal Context: Staging Records

These records are a source-faithful transcription from the retained 2025 Guangdong Statistical Yearbook ZIP archive. They provide provincial background context only.

`guangdong_population_age_structure_2015_2024.csv` reports the permanent resident population aged 0–14 and its share. It does not identify a 3–5 or 3–6 preschool-age denominator and cannot be directly used for the PRAI demand indicator.

`guangdong_fiscal_context_selected_years_2015_2024.csv` reports provincial local general public-budget revenue and expenditure for only the years published in Table 8-3: 2015 and 2020–2024. No values are created for 2016–2019. General public-budget expenditure is not a measure of preschool-specific public expenditure and cannot be directly used for the PRAI financial indicator.

Both files require independent second review before any downstream contextual analysis. They remain outside `datasets/processed/` and are not model-ready observations.
